Project 01 Active

Nuwara Eliya Highland Farm

Nuwara Eliya District, Central Province, Sri Lanka

Our flagship highland farm nestled in the cool misty terrain of Nuwara Eliya — Sri Lanka's premier vegetable-growing highland. The unique combination of volcanic soil, year-round mist, and temperatures between 5–20°C creates ideal conditions for premium potato cultivation.

Project 02 Developing

Mannar Chilli Development Site

Mannar District, Northern Province, Sri Lanka

Our developing chilli cultivation site on the arid coastal plains of Mannar. The island's low annual rainfall, intense sunshine, and sandy loam soils are internationally recognised as ideal for producing premium-heat, deeply coloured chillies for export markets.
