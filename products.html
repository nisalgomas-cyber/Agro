Highland Potato

Premium • Nuwara Eliya • Export Grade

Farm Location
Nuwara Eliya, Central Province
Product 01 · Nuwara Eliya

Potato

Ceylon Agro Development grows premium highland potatoes in the cool, misty terrains of Nuwara Eliya — Sri Lanka's highest and most fertile vegetable-growing district. At an elevation of 1,868 metres, the unique climate creates ideal natural conditions for producing firm, flavourful potatoes of excellent quality.

The volcanic highland soil delivers exceptional taste, natural texture, and a clean flavour profile that meets international export standards. Our potatoes are available fresh and graded for wholesale, retail, and export markets.

Green Chilli

Vibrant · Spicy · Mannar

Farm Location
Mannar, Northern Province
Product 02 · Mannar (Developing)

Green Chilli

Our developing Mannar farm site is planned for the cultivation of premium green chillies. Mannar's dry, arid coastal climate — with intense sun, low humidity, and sandy loam soils — is internationally recognised as ideal for producing intensely flavoured, vibrant green chillies with excellent shelf life and heat rating.

Green chillies from our Mannar site will be available fresh for immediate export, as well as processed forms such as dried and paste — catering to global food industry buyers, grocery chains, and hot sauce manufacturers.

Red Chilli

Fiery · Deep Colour · Mannar

Farm Location
Mannar, Northern Province
Product 03 · Mannar (Developing)

Red Chilli

Sri Lankan red chilli is globally prized for its deep crimson colour, robust spice profile, and superior dehydration quality. Our Mannar development site is planned to produce premium sun-dried red chillies with the intense heat and vivid colour demanded by international spice importers.

Mannar's year-round sunshine and extremely low humidity are perfect for natural sun-drying, which concentrates flavour and enhances the chilli's rich oleoresin and colour intensity. We plan to supply whole dried, flakes, fine powder, and extract to spice manufacturers worldwide.
