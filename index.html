Who We Are

Growing Sri Lanka
for Global Markets

Ceylon Agro Development Pvt Ltd is a premier Sri Lankan agribusiness dedicated to cultivating world-class agricultural produce. Founded in 2015, we bridge the gap between Sri Lanka's rich agricultural heritage and global export markets.

Our integrated farming model empowers local farming communities while delivering consistent quality to international buyers across Europe, the Middle East, and Asia.

Quality Guaranteed

GAP, HACCP & ISO compliant produce tested for international food safety standards.

Global Reach

Active export relationships in 12+ countries across EU, Middle East & Asia Pacific.

Community First

200+ farming partners supported through fair trade, training, and market access.

Our Commitment

Farming with Respect
for Earth & People

Sustainability is not an afterthought — it is at the core of everything we do. Our farming practices are designed to nourish the land, empower communities, and deliver lasting value to investors and buyers alike.

Water-Smart Irrigation

Drip irrigation and rainwater harvesting reduce water usage by up to 40%.

Organic Practices
