Our Approach

Sustainability Is Not
Optional — It's Our Core

At Ceylon Agro Development, sustainability is woven into every decision — from how we prepare the soil to how we ship the final product. We believe that doing right by the planet and doing right by our business are not competing goals.

Our approach balances modern agronomic science with traditional Sri Lankan farming wisdom, creating a model that is both productive and regenerative.

🌿
Soil Health First — We build soil organic matter through composting and cover cropping, not chemical shortcuts.
💧
Water Stewardship — Drip systems, rainwater harvesting, and ancient tank restoration reduce water footprint.
🐝
Biodiversity Protection — Pollinator corridors, hedgerows, and pesticide-free zones protect local ecosystems.
☀️
Renewable Energy — Solar panels power our packing houses and processing facilities.
How We Farm

Our Sustainable Farming
Practices

Every practice we employ is designed to maximize yield while minimizing environmental impact.

💧

Smart Water Management

Drip irrigation delivers water precisely to root zones, reducing usage by 40%. Rainwater harvesting tanks supplement irrigation across all sites. We also work with local authorities to restore ancient Sri Lankan tank irrigation systems.

🌿

Integrated Pest Management

IPM protocols use biological controls, pheromone traps, and beneficial insects to manage pests. Chemical interventions are a last resort, and we test all produce against EU maximum residue limits before packaging.

🌱

Composting & Soil Health

On-farm composting converts agricultural waste into organic fertilizer. Green manure crops and crop rotation maintain soil microbial health and reduce nutrient depletion across all our farm sites.

☀️

Solar Energy Integration

Solar panels provide 60% of energy requirements for our packing houses and processing facilities. We are investing in expanding solar capacity to achieve energy-neutral processing operations by 2026.

♻️

Waste Reduction

Organic waste from processing is composted or converted to biogas. Packaging materials are selected for recyclability. We aim for zero landfill waste from processing facilities by 2025.

🔬

Precision Agronomy

Soil testing, leaf analysis, and yield data guide every planting decision. Our agronomy team monitors crop health weekly and adjusts inputs based on actual need rather than calendar-based schedules.

People & Community

Empowering Farming
Communities

Our success is shared with the 200+ farming families who form the backbone of our supply chain.

🎓 Farmer Training Programs

Regular workshops on sustainable farming techniques, pest management, water conservation, and quality standards. 200+ farmers trained annually in partnership with the Sri Lanka Department of Agriculture.

💰 Fair & Guaranteed Pricing

Contract farming agreements guarantee fair prices above market rates for our partner farmers, eliminating middlemen and providing income security for families over multiple seasons.

🏥 Farmer Welfare Fund

Our welfare fund provides emergency health support, crop insurance coverage, and children's education scholarships for the farming families in our partner network — supporting over 800 family members.

👩‍🌾 Women in Agriculture

40% of our partner farmers and farm supervisors are women. We actively recruit and train female farmers and provide leadership opportunities within our farm management structure.

🌱 Youth Agri Initiative

Partnering with agricultural universities to provide internships and employment for young Sri Lankan agronomists — building the next generation of sustainable farming leaders.

🤝 Zero Child Labour

Absolute policy against child labour across all operations and partner farms. Regular audits ensure compliance. We support the education of partner farmers' children through our scholarship fund.
