Our Journey

A Decade of Growing
with Purpose

From a small chili farm in Kurunegala to a nationally recognized agricultural exporter.

2015

The Beginning

Ceylon Agro Development founded with 20 acres of chili cultivation in Kurunegala. First harvest exported to the UAE market.

2017

Expansion to Fruits

Added tropical fruit cultivation — papayas and mangoes. Secured first European import partnership with a German organic produce distributor.

2019

Spice Division Launch

Launched dedicated Ceylon spice operations in Matale district. Achieved GAP certification across all farm sites. Partner farmer network grew to 100+.

2021

ISO 22000 Certified

Achieved ISO 22000 food safety management certification. Expanded Polonnaruwa operations to 220 acres. Reached 10 export market countries.

2023

Sustainability Initiative

Launched comprehensive sustainability program — drip irrigation across 80% of farms, compost facilities, and farmer welfare programs.

2025

Today & Beyond

500+ acres under cultivation, 200+ farming partners, 12+ export markets. Expanding to Southeast Asia and targeting 1,000 acres by 2027.
