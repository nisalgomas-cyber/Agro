FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is your minimum order quantity for export?

Minimum order quantities vary by product. Typically, fresh produce starts at 1 full pallet (approx. 600–800kg) and dried spices/chili at 100kg. We can accommodate trial shipments for new buyers. Contact us to discuss your requirement.

What certifications do your products carry?

Our products are GlobalG.A.P., HACCP, and ISO 22000 certified. We hold phytosanitary certificates from Sri Lanka's Department of Agriculture for all fresh produce. Certificate copies available on request with each quotation.

Do you offer private label / white label services?

Yes. We offer full private label services including custom packaging design, branding, and labelling to EU, GCC, and Asian market standards. Minimum quantities apply. Please discuss your branding requirements when inquiring.

How quickly can you respond to a price request?

We typically respond to all inquiries within 24 hours during business days. For urgent export quotations, WhatsApp is the fastest channel — we usually reply within 2–4 hours.

Can I visit the farms before making a purchase or investment decision?

Absolutely. We actively encourage farm visits for serious buyers and investors. We will arrange transport from Colombo, guided tours of all relevant farm sites, and meetings with our management team. Contact us to schedule.
