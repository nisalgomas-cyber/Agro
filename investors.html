The Opportunity

Why Invest in Ceylon
Agro Development?

Global demand for sustainably sourced agricultural produce is growing at 12% per year. Sri Lanka sits at the nexus of this opportunity — fertile land, experienced farmers, and proximity to the world's fastest-growing food markets.

01

Proven Operations & Track Record

10+ years of operational history with consistent export growth, expanding buyer relationships, and a transparent supply chain that stands up to international audit.

02

High-Growth Export Market

Organic and sustainably farmed produce commands premium prices in EU, Middle East, and Asian markets. Ceylon's geographic origin adds significant brand value.

03

Government-Backed Sector

Sri Lanka's agricultural export sector benefits from government incentives, BOI status, favourable tax treatment, and strong EDB support for agribusiness investors.

04

Land Availability & Scalability

Identified expansion sites allow rapid scale-up. New technology adoption (precision ag, cold chain) creates compounding efficiency gains as investable scale increases.

05

ESG Compliant Investment

Our sustainability framework and farmer welfare programs make this a genuinely impact-positive investment aligned with ESG standards sought by global institutional capital.

06

Full Transparency & Reporting

Investors receive quarterly operational reports, farm visit access, and direct communication with management. No hidden costs, clear profit-sharing structures.

Partnership Models

How to Partner With Us

We offer flexible engagement models for investors, landowners, farming partners, and trading companies across the supply chain.

📈

Joint Venture Investment

Co-invest capital in new farm development or processing infrastructure. Structured profit-sharing with full operational transparency. Minimum investment of LKR 10M per venture.

Capital Investment Profit Sharing Board Representation
🤝

Contract Farming

Provide land or access to land — we manage all cultivation, inputs, labour, and export. You receive a guaranteed fixed return or revenue share per acre per season.

Land Contribution Fixed Returns Managed Operations
🌾

Supplier Partnership

Established farmers join our certified supply network. We provide technical training, inputs on credit, guaranteed purchase at premium prices, and export market access.

Guaranteed Offtake Technical Support Premium Pricing
🏗️

Infrastructure Investment

Fund cold storage, processing, or logistics infrastructure used across our supply chain. Return on investment through usage fees and fixed-rate lease agreements.

Asset Backed Usage Fees Long-term Lease
📦

Trading Partnership

Importer or trading houses seeking a reliable Sri Lankan origin supply partner. Exclusive territory arrangements available for high-volume buyers in key markets.

Exclusive Territories Consistent Supply Private Label
🔬

R&D & Technology Partnership

Collaborate on agricultural technology, crop research, or digital supply chain projects. Support from university partnerships and government research grants available.

Research Access Grant Support IP Sharing
